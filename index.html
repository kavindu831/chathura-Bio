Explore the Biology syllabus

Cell Biology

Start from the basic unit of life and build a strong foundation.

  • Cell structure & organelles
  • Cell membrane & transport
  • Cell division – mitosis & meiosis
  • Enzymes & metabolism

Genetics & Evolution

Understand how traits are inherited and how life changes over time.

  • DNA structure & replication
  • Protein synthesis
  • Mendelian inheritance problems
  • Evolution & natural selection

Plant Biology

From photosynthesis to plant hormones – explained simply.

  • Photosynthesis & respiration
  • Plant tissues & transport
  • Reproduction in plants
  • Plant hormones & responses

Human Biology

Learn how the body systems work together.

  • Digestive & circulatory systems
  • Respiratory & excretory systems
  • Nervous system & hormones
  • Immunity & health
